Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 24, 2026

TurboList ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Email address for account creation and authentication
  • eBay OAuth Tokens: Securely stored tokens to create listings on your behalf
  • Barcode Scans: Product barcodes you scan to look up product information
  • Subscription Status: Information about your subscription tier and usage

Device Permissions

  • Camera: Used for scanning product barcodes. We do not store photos or videos.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication and secure database storage
  • eBay API: Creating and managing your eBay listings
  • Apple StoreKit: Processing subscriptions and payments
  • Rainforest API: Retrieving product information from barcodes

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely on Firebase (Google Cloud) with industry-standard encryption. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Retention

We retain your data while your account is active. Upon account deletion, your data will be permanently removed from our systems.

Your Rights

You can request deletion of your data at any time by:

  • Using the "Disconnect eBay" feature in the app
  • Deleting your account through the app settings
  • Emailing us at the contact address below

Children's Privacy

TurboList is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

SEC. 02 / SPECIFICATIONS
FEAT-01

Parallel catalog lookup

Scans race Amazon and UPC databases in parallel, with Walmart data as a backstop. The fastest good answer wins — that’s how a draft appears in seconds, not minutes.

FEAT-02

AI item specifics

Brand, model, size, color, type — the required eBay fields filled automatically, plus AI-suggested optional specifics you can apply with one tap.

FEAT-03

AI titles & descriptions

Search-optimized titles written by AI from real product data, plus clean auto-generated descriptions. Live in the app today — not “coming soon.”

FEAT-04

Pre-flight validation

Drafts are checked against eBay’s listing rules before submission. If eBay still pushes back, TurboLister auto-fixes common rejections and explains the rest in plain English.

FEAT-05

Shopify cross-listing

Connect your Shopify store once, and eBay publishes can create the same product there automatically. One scan, two storefronts.

FEAT-06

Official eBay API

Secure OAuth through eBay’s official APIs. We never see your password, and you can revoke access any time from your eBay account.
